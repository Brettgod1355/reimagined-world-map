# Reimagined World Map

A zoomable world map made from the game's own map. It shows teleports, transport, dungeons and Sailing spots, plans a route from where you are with the teleports and items you have, finds monsters, shops and items, and shows your RuneLite party. Open it from the sidebar or with a hotkey.

The map and its data come from the plugin author's server, which sees your IP address like any website does. Nothing about you or your account is sent.
